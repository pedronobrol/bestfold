Flight simulation

Both planes are thrown with the same speed and angle and simulated live in your browser. Pick one of the 16 test throws or set your own.

A design is scored on the average of its worst 8 throws out of these 16, which vary the throw, the folding and the drag model. Every design gets the same 16 throws.

Wind tunnel

The plane is held still in a stream of air. The smoke moves with the air as the simulator computes it, including the swirl around each vortex panel. You can see the air pushed down behind the wing and the vortices trailing from the tips. Past the stall, the flow over the wing breaks up.

    Method

    Folding. Planes are only folded. Nothing is cut, taped or glued, so the folds themselves have to hold the layers together. In the model, the sheet is a set of polygons. Each fold reflects the layers on one side of a crease and restacks them. Some folds move only the top layers, which is how locks are made. A design is rejected if a fold would tear the paper, if more than eight layers meet at a crease, or if a large flap is left free to open in flight.

    Mass. Mass, balance point and inertia are computed from every folded layer of 80 g/m² paper.

    Aerodynamics. A vortex-lattice model on the wings, keel and winglets, with corrections for sharp paper edges (lost suction, and vortex lift on swept edges), skin friction, drag from thick folded edges, and stall. It is checked against wind-tunnel data for flat plates and sharp delta wings and against a measured A4 dart.

    Flight. A six-degree-of-freedom simulation in 4 ms steps, including the air the plane has to push aside. Creases act as springs, so the wings rise under load. An energy check stops any flight that gains energy.

    Trim and launch. The elevator angle and the launch angle are worked out for each plane, the way you would adjust and throw a real one.

    Search. The optimiser keeps the best plane of each type (sheet orientation, sequence of folds, winglets) and improves each with CMA-ES, spending more time on the promising types.

    Details, limitations and references are in the source repository.
