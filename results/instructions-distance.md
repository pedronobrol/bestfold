# Bestfold D-1: folding instructions

One A4 sheet, landscape, folded by hand. Step diagrams: diagrams-distance.svg

1. **Crease the centre line.** Lay the sheet landscape (long side across). Fold it in half from left to right, crease sharply, and unfold. This centre crease (21.0 cm long) is the reference for every fold that follows.
2. **Fold the corners in.** Fold the left corner inward. The crease starts at the nose tip and runs at 45.0° to the centre crease. It meets the outer edge 14.9 cm below the nose and 14.9 cm out from the centre crease. Repeat on the right so both sides match.
3. **Fold the corners in.** Fold the left corner inward. The crease starts at the nose tip and runs at 23.0° to the centre crease. It meets the outer edge 21.0 cm below the nose and 8.9 cm out from the centre crease. Repeat on the right so both sides match.
4. **Fold the nose down.** Measure 6.1 cm down from the nose along the centre crease and fold the whole nose down along a straight crease square to the centre line. The tip now lies 6.1 cm below the crease, on top.
5. **Fold in half.** Fold the plane in half along the centre crease, with the folded flaps on the inside. Crease the bottom edge (this becomes the keel) firmly.
6. **Fold the wings down.** Fold the top wing down. The crease is 0.5 cm above the keel edge at the nose and 3.7 cm above it at the tail. Mark both points and crease between them. Flip the plane over and fold the other wing to match exactly.
7. **Set the wings.** Open the wings so each sits about 3° above horizontal. Seen from behind, they form a shallow V. Pinch the keel firmly along its length so the layers stay together.
8. **Bend the elevator up.** Along the rear 0.8 cm of each wing, bend the paper up by about 12°. This is the trim tab. Test it with a gentle glide. If the plane dives, bend the trailing edge up a little. If it swoops up and stalls, bend it down a little. Repeat until it glides smoothly.
9. **Throw it.** Hold the keel 7.9 cm behind the nose, at the balance point. Throw firmly, about 11 m/s (a brisk overhand toss), aiming -4° below level with the wings level.
